01 // OUR STORY

A neighborhood bakery with big flavor.

We started with one oven, one recipe, and a stubborn belief that cake should taste like cake — not chemicals.

Sweet Crumbs opened its doors in 2012 in a tiny corner shop. Today, we bake over 40 varieties of cakes, pastries and breads — all from scratch, every single morning. No mixes, no shortcuts, no artificial anything.
